Privacy Policy for Ebb & Flow

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Ebb & Flow ("the app") shows tides, weather and sea conditions for beaches, lets you keep a private journal of your days at the beach, and lets signed-in users share short beach reports and comments with each other (the Community pages). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who receives it, and what you can do about it.

What the app uses

You can use the tide and weather screens without an account

Without an account we do not know who you are. We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and the app collects usage statistics only if you say yes (see "Optional anonymous usage data" below).

Optional anonymous usage data (off unless you say yes)

After you have used the app a few times it asks once whether you want to share anonymous usage data, and you can change your answer at any time in Profile > Privacy ("Share anonymous usage data"). Until you say yes, nothing is sent. If you say yes, the app sends which screens and features are used (for example "opened the Map", "saved a beach", the beach's id and the type of device event) so we can see what is useful and what is not. These events carry a random identifier created on your phone. They are not linked to your account, name, email or Apple/Google ID, and never include your location, your posts, your journal or your photos. Our analytics provider is set not to store your IP address with these events. They are kept for up to one year. When you switch the setting off, the app stops sending and deletes the random identifier on your phone (events already sent cannot be tied to you, because they carry no account information). The provider is PostHog (EU cloud, servers in the European Union).

Your location stays on your phone

If you allow it, the app uses your phone's approximate location (about 1 km) only to sort beaches by distance. Your location is not sent to our servers and not stored. You can switch this off at any time in iOS Settings.

Your journal stays on your phone

The journal (entries, the text you write, the beach and time, the tide height at that time, your rating and activities, and the photos you add) is stored only on your phone. It is not sent to our servers or to anyone else, it is not tied to an account (you can keep a journal without one), and we cannot see it or restore it. - Photos: you choose the pictures in Apple's photo picker, which gives the app only the pictures you pick (the app cannot browse your library and asks for no photo permission). The app saves a smaller copy inside its own storage, without the location and camera details that may be in the original; your original stays untouched in your Photos library. - Protection: the journal files are protected by iOS while your phone is locked. - Backups: if you back up your phone (to iCloud or a computer), the journal can be part of that backup. That is handled by Apple under your own settings, not by us. - Exports: the Journal can create a PDF, a text file or a ZIP with your photos. Nothing is uploaded: the file is handed to the iOS share sheet and goes where you choose (Files, AirDrop, Mail and so on). Once shared, it is under the control of the place you sent it to. - Removal: you can delete an entry in the Journal (its photos are deleted with it). Deleting the app deletes the journal from your phone. Deleting your account does not touch the journal, because it is not in your account.

What is sent when you look at conditions

To show tides, weather and sea conditions the app asks public data services for the coordinates of the beach you are viewing (rounded), not your own location: Open-Meteo (worldwide weather, waves, sea temperature and a tide model), and in the United States also NOAA (tides, water temperature), the National Weather Service and the National Data Buoy Center. Like every internet request, these requests reveal your phone's IP address to the service. We do not send them your name, email or account details. See each service's own privacy policy for how they handle requests.

Who processes your information for us

  • Supabase (database, accounts, file storage and server functions). Our project is hosted in the United States (AWS us-west-2). Supabase provides a data processing agreement that includes standard contractual clauses for transfers from the EU, UK and Switzerland.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and push notifications (Apple Push Notification service).
  • Google: Sign in with Google, only if you choose it.
  • PostHog (usage statistics, EU cloud), only if you choose to share anonymous usage data.
  • Open-Meteo, NOAA, National Weather Service, National Data Buoy Center: public data services, described above.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers.

Legal bases (people in the EU, UK and similar regions)

  • To provide the app and your account (contract): account, community features, favourites, notifications you request.
  • Our legitimate interests in keeping the community safe and the service secure: moderation, reports, blocking, abuse prevention, filtering of offensive content and contact details in posts.
  • Your consent: push notifications, the use of your location (withdraw at any time in iOS Settings) and anonymous usage data (withdraw at any time in Profile > Privacy).

Public content

Your display name, profile photo, posts and comments can be seen by other users of the Community pages. Do not post personal information you do not want others to see. Posts and comments that break the Community Guidelines can be removed, and accounts can be suspended.

How long we keep it

Your journal is kept on your phone until you delete the entries or the app. We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (Profile > Delete Account) we delete your profile, posts, comments, likes, favourites, notification settings, push-notification addresses, feedback, reports and blocks, and your profile photo files, and for Sign in with Apple we revoke the connection at Apple. Routine backups and server logs kept by our hosting provider are overwritten on the provider's own schedule, which is a matter of days to weeks.

Your rights

  • Get a copy of your data: Profile > Account > Download My Data gives you a file with the information the app stores about your account, including your posts and comments. Your journal is not part of that file because it never leaves your phone: use the export in the Journal.
  • Delete your data: Profile > Delete Account (account data), delete entries in the Journal or delete the app (journal).
  • Correct your data: change your display name and photo in Profile.
  • If you are in the EU, UK or a similar region you can also object to or ask us to restrict processing, and you can complain to your local data-protection authority. If you live in California, we do not sell or share personal information for advertising.
  • To use a right the app does not cover, write to alex.jambor.n@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to create an account. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our servers are in the United States. If you use the app from another country, your account information is transferred to and stored in the United States. The safeguard we rely on is the standard contractual clauses in our hosting provider's data processing agreement.

Security

Data is sent encrypted and each account can only change its own content. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, where required, tell you in the app.
