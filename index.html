Ebb & Flow Support

Ebb & Flow shows tides, weather and sea conditions for beaches around the world, with a map, a private journal and a community page for each beach.

Contact: alex.jambor.n@gmail.com. We answer within a few days, and reports about content in the Community pages within 24 hours.

Common questions

Where do the tide and weather numbers come from?

Tides come from NOAA (United States) and from a tide model for other places; weather and sea conditions come from the National Weather Service, the National Data Buoy Center and Open-Meteo. Values are estimates and can be wrong. Do not use them for navigation or safety decisions. The full list is in the app under Profile > Data Sources & Credits.

A beach has no tide data, or the tide looks wrong.

Tide predictions need a nearby measuring station or enough tide range for the model. Where neither exists the app says so instead of guessing. Tell us the beach name and what you saw (Profile > Provide Feedback, or write to the address above).

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile, scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account. This removes your profile, posts, comments, likes, saved beaches, notification settings and feedback from our servers, and your profile photo. Your journal is stored only on your phone and is not part of your account.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Profile > Account > Download My Data. The journal has its own export (PDF, text or ZIP) inside the Journal tab.

What happens to my journal if I delete the app or change phones?

The journal never leaves your phone, so we cannot restore it. Back up your phone (iCloud or a computer) and use the Journal export to keep a copy.

How do I stop notifications?

Profile > Activity > Notifications in the app, or iOS Settings > Notifications > Ebb & Flow.

How do I report a post or a person?

Tap the menu on a post or comment and choose Report, or Block to hide that person. Community rules are in Profile > Terms & Community Guidelines. We review reports and remove content that breaks the rules.

Anonymous usage data

The app asks once whether you want to share anonymous usage data. You can change the answer at any time in Profile > Privacy.
